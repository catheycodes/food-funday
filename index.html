Our story

Great food meets nature-inspired calm

Green Multicuisine Restaurant serves 300+ dishes from around the world — South Indian parathas and kothu varieties, Chinese, Continental, desserts, and refreshing beverages, all from one kitchen.

Step into a green-themed room built to slow you down, whether you've come with family, caught up with old friends, or you're marking something worth celebrating.

Where great food meets a one-of-a-kind nature-inspired experience — that's the Green promise.
